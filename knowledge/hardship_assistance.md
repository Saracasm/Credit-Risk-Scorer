# Northwind Bank — Hardship & Payment Assistance (Sample)

> Fictional sample hardship policy for demonstrating retrieval-augmented advising.

## When to Offer Hardship Options

Applicants or existing borrowers showing recent late payments, high utilization,
or rising debt-to-income may be experiencing temporary hardship (job loss,
medical event). The advisor should mention available assistance rather than only
focusing on decline reasons.

## Available Assistance Programs

- **Payment deferral:** move one or two payments to the end of the loan term for
  borrowers who are current but anticipate a short-term gap.
- **Reduced-payment plan:** temporarily lower monthly payments for 3–6 months
  for documented hardship.
- **Interest-rate reduction:** for borrowers in good standing facing rate-driven
  affordability issues.
- **Loan modification:** extend the term to lower the monthly payment when
  hardship is expected to last longer.

## Eligibility

- Borrower must document the hardship (e.g., layoff notice, medical bills).
- Programs are intended to prevent default, not to extend new credit.
- Enrolling in an assistance plan does not erase existing late marks, but
  prevents new ones from accruing during the plan.

## How This Affects Advising

When an applicant's profile shows recent delinquency, explain that bringing
accounts current is the top priority and that hardship programs exist to help
avoid further damage. This is more constructive than simply flagging the
delinquency as a risk factor.
