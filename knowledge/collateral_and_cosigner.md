# Northwind Bank — Collateral, Co-Signer & Secured Credit (Sample)

> Fictional sample policy for demonstrating retrieval-augmented advising.

## When a Co-Signer Is Required or Recommended

- **Subprime (PD 40%–60%):** a co-signer is *recommended* and can move pricing
  toward the better end of the tier's range.
- **Deep Subprime (PD above 60%):** a co-signer is *required* for any unsecured
  product.
- The co-signer must independently meet at least Near-Prime criteria
  (utilization at or below 30%, debt-to-income at or below 0.40, no 90+ day
  lates).

## Collateral Options

- Secured products (auto loans, HELOCs, secured cards) reduce the bank's risk
  and can make borderline applicants eligible.
- Acceptable collateral: financed vehicles, real estate, or a cash deposit.
- Collateral value is discounted (loan-to-value caps apply by tier) to leave a
  safety margin.

## Secured Credit Card

A practical path for applicants who do not yet qualify for unsecured credit.

- Secured by a refundable cash deposit (typically $200–$2,000) that sets the
  credit limit.
- Reports to credit bureaus, so responsible use rebuilds the payment history.
- After 12 months of on-time payments, the account may be reviewed for an
  upgrade to an unsecured card and return of the deposit.

## Advising Guidance

For applicants who fall short of unsecured eligibility, present collateral, a
qualified co-signer, or a secured product as constructive paths forward rather
than ending the conversation at "declined."
