# Northwind Bank — Loan Product Catalog (Sample)

> Fictional sample product catalog for demonstrating retrieval-augmented advising.

## Personal Installment Loan

Unsecured fixed-rate loan for general purposes.

- Amounts: $2,000–$50,000.
- Terms: 24–60 months.
- Available to **Prime and Near-Prime** tiers without collateral; Subprime
  requires a co-signer or collateral.
- APR follows the tier pricing in the lending policy (5.5%–18% depending on tier).
- No prepayment penalty.

## Auto Loan

Secured by the financed vehicle.

- Amounts: up to 100% of vehicle value for Prime, 90% for Near-Prime, 80% for
  Subprime.
- Terms: 36–72 months.
- The vehicle serves as collateral, so Subprime applicants may still qualify
  without a separate co-signer.

## Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC)

Revolving credit secured by real estate.

- Requires at least one real estate property on file and a debt-to-income ratio
  at or below 0.43.
- Available to Prime and Near-Prime tiers only.
- Variable rate tied to the prime rate.

## Credit Builder Loan

Designed for applicants in the **Deep Subprime** band or with thin/no credit
history who want to establish a payment record.

- Amounts: $500–$2,500 held in a locked savings account and released as the
  loan is repaid.
- Reports on-time payments to build history.
- No minimum credit score; income sufficient to cover the payment is required.

## Choosing a Product

Match the product to the applicant's tier and goal: rebuilding credit → Credit
Builder Loan or secured card; general financing with strong credit → Personal
Installment Loan; vehicle purchase → Auto Loan. Never promise approval — present
options the applicant's tier is eligible for and refer to underwriting.
