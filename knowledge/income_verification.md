# Northwind Bank — Income & Documentation Requirements (Sample)

> Fictional sample documentation policy for demonstrating retrieval-augmented advising.

## Standard Documentation by Tier

- **Prime:** most recent pay stub or one year of tax returns; no employment
  verification call required.
- **Near-Prime:** two most recent pay stubs plus a six-month employment
  verification (the bank may call the employer).
- **Subprime / Deep Subprime:** three months of pay stubs or bank statements,
  plus proof of stable residence; self-employed applicants provide two years of
  tax returns.

## Acceptable Proof of Income

- W-2 wage earners: recent pay stubs or an employer letter.
- Self-employed: two years of filed tax returns and recent bank statements.
- Fixed income (pensions, benefits): award letters or annual statements.
- Additional income (rental, part-time): documented and recurring for at least
  12 months to be counted toward debt-to-income.

## Why Documentation Matters

Verified income directly affects the debt-to-income ratio and the maximum
payment calculation (capped at 28% of disposable income). Unverifiable income
cannot be counted, which can lower the eligible loan amount. Advise applicants
to gather these documents before applying to avoid delays.

## Processing Timelines

- Prime applications: typically decisioned within one business day once
  documents are received.
- Near-Prime and below: 2–5 business days, longer if employment verification or
  additional documents are pending.
